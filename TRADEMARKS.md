# BeepBoopBit trademark policy

The BeepBoopBit name, the BBB name when identifying this design system, and the
lowercase **bbb.** wordmark identify the official BeepBoopBit project and its
products. This policy explains permitted uses of these marks and helps people
distinguish official releases from independent projects.

## Software reuse

BBB source is available under the [MIT license](LICENSE). You may use, copy,
modify, distribute, and sell the components, CLI, styles, tokens, and brand preset
code under that license, including in commercial products. You do not need
trademark permission to reuse the software or its colors and visual styles.

This policy concerns use of the names and wordmark as brand identifiers. It does
not add conditions to the MIT license, revoke copyright permissions already
granted, or restrict uses permitted by applicable law, including fair or
nominative use. Third-party materials remain subject to their own licenses.

## Uses allowed without asking

You may use the marks to refer truthfully to the official project, provided the
use does not suggest sponsorship, endorsement, or affiliation that does not
exist. For example, you may:

- Credit the project with wording such as "Built using the BBB design system."
- Describe compatibility, such as "A theme for the BBB design system."
- Discuss, review, compare, or criticize BeepBoopBit and BBB in documentation,
  tutorials, articles, presentations, and other publications.
- Display the unmodified wordmark to identify or link to the official project,
  with your own product's identity clearly distinguished.
- Include screenshots of the project when demonstrating or discussing it.

These permissions apply to commercial and non-commercial uses alike. No visible
"powered by" badge or public endorsement is required when using the software;
retain the notices required by the applicable software licenses.

## Uses requiring written permission

Except where permitted by applicable law, obtain written permission before:

- Using the marks as the name or primary branding of a separate product, service,
  fork, organization, domain, or social account.
- Presenting a product, service, or modified distribution as an official
  BeepBoopBit release, or as sponsored, endorsed, certified, or affiliated with
  BeepBoopBit.
- Using a modified wordmark or a confusingly similar mark as your product's
  identity.
- Selling merchandise that features the marks as its branding.

When publishing an independent fork or product, give it its own name and branding
so people can distinguish it from the official project. Accurate attribution to
BBB remains welcome. You may keep technical identifiers such as `--bbb-*` CSS
variables, `bbb.json`, source directory names, and references to
`@beepboopbit/ui`; you do not need to rename them to reuse the code.

## Permission requests

Contact the BeepBoopBit maintainers through the
[official repository](https://github.com/BeepBoopBit/bbb-design) with a description
of the proposed use and an example of how the name or wordmark will appear.
Permission applies to the use approved in writing; a lack of response is not
approval.

For background on the distinction between software licensing and project identity,
see the [Open Source Initiative FAQ](https://opensource.org/faq).
